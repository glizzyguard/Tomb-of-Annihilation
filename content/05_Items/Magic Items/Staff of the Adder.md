# Staff of the Adder

The **Staff of the Adder** was a magic staff taken from the [[Snake Temple]] and carried by [[Taenae]], who later sold it.[^s3]

## Powers

At a command word the head of the staff became that of a living poisonous snake for a minute, and the same word returned it to its ordinary form. While it lived the head could strike, and its bite was poisonous. The head could itself be attacked, and if it was killed the staff was destroyed.

## History

The party took the staff from the Snake Temple in the [[Aldani Basin]] on the fifth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, after it stopped the ritual of the [[Red Wizards]] there.[^s3] Taenae took it and later sold it. When and to whom is not recorded.
