# Folding Boat

The **Folding Boat** was a wooden box that unfolded into a boat or a ship at a command word. The party took it from the hoard at the top of [[Firefinger]].[^s8]

## Description

Folded, it was a wooden box about a foot long and half as wide and deep, heavy for its size. It unfolded into either of two vessels: a boat ten feet long with a single pair of oars, or a ship twenty-four feet long with a deck, a mast and sail, rowing seats, oars, a steering oar and an anchor. The wood was etched with Aarakocra symbols for "Wind" and "Safe Passage."

## Powers

One command word unfolded the box into the boat, a second into the ship, and a third folded it back into a box, which worked only when no one was aboard.

## History

The party took the Folding Boat from the hoard at the top of Firefinger on the twenty-eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, after defeating the Pterafolk there.[^s8] It replaced the canoe that had been destroyed on the [[Tiryki River]] on the seventh.[^s5] The party used it from then on whenever it traveled by river.
