# Bag of Holding

A **Bag of Holding** was a bag whose inside was far larger than its outside. Every member of the party carried one, and the party found another on [[Firefinger]].[^s8]

## Description

The bag was about two feet across at the mouth and four feet deep, and weighed the same whatever was put into it.

## Powers

The bag held far more than its size allowed. If it was overloaded, pierced or torn it burst and was destroyed, and its contents were scattered in the Astral Plane. Turned inside out, it spilled its contents unharmed and had to be put right before it could be used again. A breathing creature could survive inside it only for some minutes. If the bag was placed inside another space of the same kind, both were destroyed and a gate opened to the Astral Plane, which pulled in anything nearby and then closed for good.

## History

The party took a Bag of Holding from the hoard at the top of Firefinger on the twenty-eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.[^s8]

## Appendix

### Notes
