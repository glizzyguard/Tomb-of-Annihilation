# Wingclipper

**Wingclipper** was a greatsword of obsidian and bone that [[Takhesis Calva]] carried from [[Firefinger]] until he took up [[Vigil's End]].

## Description

The blade was of black obsidian reinforced with bone, and carvings ran its length that showed winged hunters descending on their prey. The hilt was wrapped in weathered leather, with the feathers of exotic birds tied to the pommel. The blade glinted with an unnatural sharpness, and one who held it could hear a faint whistling of wind even in a quiet room.

## Powers

Wingclipper cut deeper into creatures that flew. Once between rests its bearer could call up the heat of the beacon fire it had guarded, and for a minute the blade gave off warmth and a faint light and burned what it struck.

## History

The sword was forged by artisans who revered the skies, and the Pterafolk took it as a relic of conquest.

Takhesis claimed it at the top of Firefinger, after the party defeated the Pterafolk there on the twenty-seventh and twenty-eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.[^s8] He fought with it until the twenty-eighth of Flamerule, when he came back from [[Skyfang Keep]] with Vigil's End.[^s35] It was destroyed in the Cradle with the rest of what he carried.
