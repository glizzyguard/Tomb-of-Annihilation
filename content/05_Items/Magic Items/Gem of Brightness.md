# Gem of Brightness

The **Gem of Brightness** was a prism that threw light. The party took it from the [[Snake Temple]] in the [[Aldani Basin]].[^s3]

## Description

The gem was a prism.

## Powers

The gem answered to three command words. The first made it shed bright light around its holder. The second sent a beam of brilliant light at one creature, which could be blinded by it for a time. The third made the gem flare in a blinding cone before it. The beam and the flare drew on a store of power in the gem, and when the store was spent the gem became an ordinary jewel.

## History

The party took the gem from the Snake Temple on the fifth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, after it stopped the ritual of the [[Red Wizards]] there, together with the [[Staff of the Adder]] and the [[Cloak of Billowing]].[^s3] It needed no attunement, and the party never used it.
