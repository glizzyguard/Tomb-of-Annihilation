# Quaal's Feather Token (Bird)

**Quaal's Feather Token (Bird)** was a magic token in the shape of a feather that the party found on [[Firefinger]] and never used.[^s8]

## Description

The token looked like a feather.

## Powers

The token could be used once. Tossed into the air, it vanished and an enormous, many-colored bird took its place. The bird could not attack. It obeyed simple commands and could carry a heavy load through the air for a day's flight, after which it disappeared.

## History

The party took the token from the hoard at the top of Firefinger on the twenty-eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.[^s8] It was never used in the course of the expedition.
