# The Cradle of the Death God

**The Cradle of the Death God**, called the Cradle, was the lowest chamber of the [[Tomb of the Nine Gods]], where [[The Atropal|the Atropal]] fed on the Soulmonger, the source of the [[The Death Curse|Death Curse]], above a lake of lava.[^s80][^s89] The party came down to it on the afternoon of the sixteenth of Eleasis, 1492 DR, and fought [[Takhesis Calva]] there, the vessel of the lich [[Acererak]].[^s89][^s90] The fight ended the Death Curse and cost the lives of [[Raiketsu]], [[Inete]] and Takhesis, and the chamber was destroyed with the Tomb and the city of [[Omu]] above it.[^s89][^s90]

> [!quote] Takhesis Calva, to the party at the end of the fight
> _"No. The Atropal is dying because I am dying. If you save me, you save it. Don't be cruel."_[^s90]

## Description

The party entered on a balcony over a lake of fire. A crystal cylinder, the Soulmonger, stood over the lake on three adamantine struts, and the Atropal hung above it and fed. A mask of Acererak the size of a doorway hung in the chamber.[^s89]

There were other balconies, among them one at the back left and one on the south side, as well as a platform and a ledge.[^s89][^s90] The lake was lava. When the Soulmonger fell into it the chamber went dark, and late in the fight the surface crusted gray-black and bore the weight of those who fought on it.[^s89][^s90]

## Geography

The Cradle lay below the [[Level 6 - The Mothers' Lair|sixth level]] of the Tomb. A black staircase led down to it from the green gate of that level, with masks set in its walls every ten steps, each the same ivory face angled at the stairs.[^s88][^s89] The party had come four hundred feet down to reach it.[^s89] The gate at the top of the stairs ground shut behind the paladin [[Savriel Tirinath]], the last to come down.[^s89]

## Inhabitants

The Atropal hung above the Soulmonger and was joined to it by a cord. It had a second cord, which struck Takhesis in the breastbone.[^s89] Savriel called it the child.[^s89][^s90] Takhesis had seen it earlier in a vision as an unborn child inside a monstrous heart of ice.[^s50]

Takhesis Calva, a Fallen Aasimar who had traveled with the party until the fourteenth of Eleasis, was Acererak's vessel. The [[Sewn Sisters]] had carried him down into the Tomb that morning with the words _"Welcome home, our perfect child... the cradle is waiting."_[^s70] He was not in sight when the party arrived, and he came out of a shadow in the chamber.[^s89]

Five dead of the Calva clan rose out of Takhesis at his call: Grathar, Nimue, Cyra, Naeleen and his sister [[Rani Calva]]. They are told under [[The Calva Dead]].[^s89]

The souls came out in a flood when the Soulmonger fell.[^s89] Acererak himself did not appear. His mask never moved, and his name was not spoken in the chamber.[^s90]

## Notable locations

- The balcony at the foot of the stairs, where the party came in and where Takhesis died.[^s89][^s90]
- The Soulmonger, on its three adamantine struts over the lake.[^s89]
- The back-left balcony, where a squat dark jar stood. It was the phylactery of the lich [[Valindra Shadowmantle]]. One of the three struts stood on the same side.[^s89]
- The span of the back-right strut, where Rani Calva stood last of her family.[^s89]
- The south balcony, where an arch woke when the Atropal burst.[^s90]
- The mask of Acererak.[^s89]

## History

### Before the party

On the fifth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, the party learned that the cause of the Death Curse was a necrotic artifact called the Soulmonger, and the priest [[Zitembe]] confirmed it on the seventh.[^s3][^s5] Zitembe's journal said of himself and [[Taban Veilthorn]] that if they died as mortals the Atropal would feast on seven hundred years of their souls.[^s33] The last words of [[Syndra Silvane]], as the merchant prince [[Wakanga O'tamu]] reported them, were _"Tell them the Atropal laughs."_[^s36]

On the night of the twelfth of Eleasis Valindra Shadowmantle made it her price that [[Vonara]] retrieve the Soulmonger, or a specific artifact, from the Tomb for her.[^s59] On the fifteenth Valindra reminded her, through the geas that bound her, that she was to deliver the Soulmonger intact, and before dawn on the sixteenth the geas showed Vonara a hazy image of a jar.[^s80][^s86]

Inside the Tomb the party learned more of what lay at the bottom. On the third level shadows showed Inete, [[Vogal]] and [[Taenae]] visions of their own deaths, which were premonitions of the fight with Takhesis and the Atropal.[^s73] The beholder [[Belchorzh]], dying, called the party midwives who were helping to bring something unholy into the world.[^s78] Savriel, performing a rite for the dead [[Ersklan Ghostfang]], found that the soul of everyone who died in the Tomb was held conscious and dragged down to feed whatever was at the bottom.[^s80] From the night of the fifteenth [[Glade]] carried the [[Heartwell Vial]], whose beat followed the nearness of the Soulmonger.[^s83]

### The descent

On the afternoon of the sixteenth of Eleasis, the eighty-fourth day of the expedition, Taenae, Vogal, Vonara, Glade, Inete and the Goliath monk Raiketsu opened the green gate of the sixth level. Acererak, whom the party knew as the Architect, showed each of them a vision of their own death, and Raiketsu's reached all six. They went down the black staircase about sixty hours after entering the Tomb.[^s88] The party believed that it was walking to meet the Architect.[^s89]

The chamber held no one but the Atropal. Taenae, from Glade's explanation and her own reasoning, worked out that the Soulmonger and the Atropal were the source of the Death Curse. It was the party's first confirmation of what it had come to break. No one attacked the Atropal.[^s89]

The Heartwell Vial stopped pointing down and hammered at its glass. Vogal's [[Belt of Orckind]] grew warm. The geas turned Vonara's head to the left, and her eyes landed on a squat dark vessel on the back-left balcony. She named it for what it was, the thing she was bound to carry out.[^s89]

Savriel then stepped out of the dark at the top of the stairs, in silver plate, with the sword _Anar'vandil_ in her hand and the vipers of her hair awake, and the gate ground shut behind her. The [[Black Opal Crown]] was on her brow, and no one in the party remarked on it.[^s89] The party had left her at the control panel of the fifth level, and did not know that [[Withers]], the Tomb's custodian, had taken her from it.[^s88] Asked how she had come, she said that Withers had sent her. Asked further, she gave the party his name, Gorra. She carried a letter sealed with a W and addressed to Taenae. It spoke of the office fire and the returned bird, and told her to _"hold nothing back."_ Taenae read it aloud and kept it.[^s89]

### The death of Raiketsu and the return of Takhesis

Raiketsu punched one of the struts. A crack ran eight feet up the adamantine, the chamber rang, and the Atropal turned its head, its second cord pointing down the room at the sound. A shadow formed behind Raiketsu. He went to one knee and said, _"I've seen this before…"_ It was the death he had been shown at the gate. Taenae tried to reach him, Vonara mounted her broom, Inete and Vogal stood ready, and Glade cast _telekinesis_. None of it was fast enough. A hand closed on the crown of his head and crushed his skull.[^s89]

No one in the party had spoken a name for the figure. The shadow thickened and turned, and showed black hair, the Calva crest and, for one heartbeat, ice-blue eyes before starlight filled them. Taenae said, _"Tak? Is that you???"_[^s89]

Standing over the body, Takhesis told the party that for eighty-four days they had been spectators inside his Grand Design and had understood none of it, that they chose only now to oppose what they could not fathom, and that the Design finished that night. The words were wholly his own, and Acererak did not speak through him. A blue-white fire rose out of Raiketsu's body with the shape of a kamadan coiled around it, which was the Trickster God [[Shagambi]]. Both lights went into Takhesis. The Atropal's second cord struck his breastbone, the Atropal giggled, and he grew to twenty feet, the griffon of his crest across a chest the size of a gate. The air grew ten degrees colder.[^s89]

A wail followed, and a wave of necrotic energy rolled down the chamber. The party had braced for it better than it had for the shadow. Vogal went into his rage.[^s89]

Savriel drove the rim of her shield into the stone and raised a canopy of five gold ribs across the chamber. She told the party that she could hold back the child, and only the child, and that they must stop whatever Takhesis was becoming.[^s89]

### The Calva dead and the struts

The Trickster Gods rose in those who carried them, with the words _"Not another. Stand with us."_ Each gave its host strength and a voice. Taenae led, as the letter had told her to. [[Chakuna]] then called her favor, and Taenae became the Avatar of Chakuna in the shape of the Queen Displacer Beast, of huge size, her six legs cracking the platform, the light of the lava bending around her as it bent around Takhesis. The god [[Nangnang]], who had ridden in her, fled as a small green light with a scream like a prey animal's, which was cut off as it dived into the pearl in her pack.[^s89]

Takhesis did not raise his sword. He put his hand on the griffon and called five names: Grathar, Nimue, Cyra, Naeleen and Rani. Five shapes pulled themselves out of him and took their stations at the struts. Rani said to the Avatar and then to the room, _"We failed him once. We will not fail him twice. Forgive us."_ The other four never spoke.[^s89]

Taenae attacked Takhesis directly and found that the harm passed down into the strut beneath him. That strut, which was Rani's, was the first to fall. Taenae and Glade together broke the second, the one on the balcony on the jar's side. During the fight Vonara took the jar, and it was in her pack before the third strut fell.[^s89]

Grathar, Cyra, Nimue and Naeleen fell one after another in that order, each of them relieved. Rani did not fall, and stood last on the span of the back-right strut.[^s89]

Twice Takhesis struck at Savriel with rays, and each time a rib of the canopy went out. The other ribs failed one by one. The party saw the end of the canopy coming and threw everything at the last strut. Savriel said upward, to the Atropal, _"I know. I know it hurts. It's going to be okay."_ The party saw her strain. When the last rib broke she collapsed in a heap.[^s89]

With the canopy gone, all five of the party were struck at once by a blow from the Atropal. The strength the gods had lent them tried to take the blow, but it dug deeper and wounded their souls directly, reducing their life force while the Atropal was still alive.[^s89][^s90][^s91]

### The fall of the Soulmonger

The third strut then broke. Who broke it is not recorded. The Soulmonger dropped, and the Atropal was dragged down with it by its own cord. The lava took them both, the chamber went dark, and the souls came out in a flood. A heartbeat went on under the lake. None of the party had fallen.[^s89]

The cord tore out of Takhesis's chest and he fell. The ichor followed him down, and wings of black ice caught him ten feet above the lava. [[Vigil's End]], his greatsword, came off his back, and he smiled. He said, _"Now the fun begins."_[^s89]

### The fight on the wing

Fear spread from his first wingbeat, and of the party only Glade was frightened. Rani came off the strut span and set herself in front of him over thirty feet of open air, with both scimitars and her wings spread wide enough to cover him.[^s90]

The gods answered again with less than before, and their strength did not reach the Avatar. Vonara gave the whole of her own share to Taenae.[^s90]

When Rani fell she lowered her bent scimitars, looked at her brother and said, _"Brother. Wake up."_ Everything the family had poured into her passed into him at once. His eyes cleared, and then the starlight settled back.[^s90]

Early in this part of the fight Glade and Inete each cast _disintegrate_ at him, and he resisted both. At length his wings shattered. The lava crusted gray-black to catch him, and for one breath his eyes were blue.[^s90]

### The fight on the crust

On the crust Takhesis cast _disrupt life_, which stripped from the party the last of what the gods had lent them. The party saw that fire harmed him more than anything else and turned to it.[^s90]

The Avatar fell to Vigil's End. The Queen Displacer Beast came apart, its shadow pouring upward through the stone, and Taenae was left kneeling where it had stood, in her own body, exhausted and close to death. She then healed Savriel.[^s90]

Takhesis lifted Vogal by a pull on his soul, held him, and struck at him while he hung there. Vogal was struck down twice, and both times his rage put him back on his feet. He never fell.[^s90]

### The deaths of Inete and Takhesis

Every one of the party was by then close to falling. Takhesis said, _"I'm not going down alone."_ He seized Taenae with the same pull, rushed Inete and beheaded her with one swing. Glade cast _telekinesis_ again and lifted her body, and he carried her from then on.[^s90]

Takhesis took another step and his knees buckled. He caught himself on Vigil's End, put his hand to the griffon and called, and nothing rose. One of the party said, _"We are your family too…"_ He collapsed, and Taenae was released. The frost stopped and the sword fell.[^s90]

He told the party not to save him, because the Atropal was dying as he died and to save him was to save it. He set the point of the sword against his own chest and said that if they would not do it, he would. Vogal accepted and killed him, remarking as he did so that this was the second time he had had to kill a sworn brother. The starlight went out of Takhesis's eyes, and they were blue for the final time.[^s90]

The gods left their hosts in the quiet that followed, without a word, one by one: [[Obo'laka]], [[Unkh]], [[Kubazan]] and [[Wongo]]. Nangnang stayed in the pearl, which was warm in Taenae's pack. Vogal took Vigil's End and a kneecap from the body.[^s90]

### The destruction and the escape

The heartbeat under the crust stumbled, and the floor screamed, high, thin and like a child. Cracks ran outward from beneath the body. The party was already on the ledge. Savriel said that the child was dying with Takhesis and that this could be very bad.[^s90]

The wail stopped, the flood of souls ran to its last lights, and the Death Curse was over.[^s90]

The Atropal then burst. A sphere of white grew out of the lake and ate the chamber from the middle outward, and the arch on the south balcony woke. The party ran. Vonara carried Vogal and Taenae on her broom, and Glade carried himself and Inete's body by _telekinesis_, through the arch. The party knew that Savriel meant to stay, and that there was no time to stop her or to take Takhesis's body.[^s90]

Each of them had one breath. Vonara looked back at Savriel. Vogal thought of the brothers he had lost over the adventure. Taenae reached [[Alice]] through their bond. Alice saw what was happening, and those who waited in Omu left the city before the sphere came up the canyon. What Glade did is not recorded. Savriel watched the party from her knees and did not speak.[^s90]

Savriel stayed behind and was lost, and the Black Opal Crown and _Anar'vandil_ with her. Raiketsu's body stayed in the chamber, and the sphere took the body of Takhesis. The body of [[The Boy|the Boy]], on the sixth level, was lost with the rest of the Tomb. Omu sank, deepened and became a caldera.[^s90]

The party woke on wet leaves in the jungle before dawn on the seventeenth of Eleasis, the eighty-fifth day of the expedition: Vogal, Glade, Vonara and Taenae alive, and Inete dead in Glade's arms. The Heartwell Vial beat once, hugely, and stopped. The death it had drunk went out to join the light, one golden shape looked back at Glade, and the orchid he wore opened every petal.[^s90]

The party brought out of the Cradle the jar, Vigil's End and the kneecap, Withers' letter, the pearl, Inete's body and head, the five gifts of the Boy, three Goblin fingers that the Sewn Sisters had returned to Vogal, and the lizard [[King]].[^s88][^s90]

### Aftermath

At dawn the survivors came to a ridge. Below it, where Omu, the Tomb and the canyon had been, was a bowl of red earth two miles wide, still smoking. Figures on the far ridge raised their hands. Everyone who had waited for the party in Omu had lived. Taenae took the shape of a quetzalcoatlus and flew the party and Inete's body across to their camp.[^s91]

That morning [[Sister Silas]] cast _resurrection_ over Inete, and Inete chose to return. It was the first resurrection to work in a year. As she came back, the farewell of Obo'laka came to her, which the god had left unsaid in the Cradle.[^s90][^s91] Vonara told the camp the story of the Tomb, leaving out that Takhesis had turned against them, and told them that the Death Curse had been dealt with at its root.[^s91]
