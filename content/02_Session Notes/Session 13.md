# Session 13: The Book of Wellness & The Hungerer

From the edge of the [[Aldani Basin]] the party returned to [[Camp Vengeance]], where [[Niles Breakbone|Commander Breakbone]] set them to save the missing scout [[Lorsa Rygor]]. [[Taenae]], [[Glade]] and [[Vogal]] wrote a guide to hygiene, the Book of Wellness, and Glade made the camp a sewer. Guided by [[Wulf Rygor]], the party found the abandoned [[Aldani Shrine]], destroyed the undead attacking Lorsa's barricade and heard a warning that the Hungerer was coming.

## Recap

### Camp Vengeance

The party has just teleported by Glade's magic from the cliffs of [[Kir Sabal]] to the edge of the Aldani Basin. On the morning of the thirtieth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, they enter Camp Vengeance and reunite with [[Adam]], a guard.

They meet with Commander Breakbone. The task is to save the camp's best scout, Lorsa Rygor, the wife of Wulf Rygor, who went missing near an abandoned shrine.

### The sanitation crusade

Taenae, Glade and Vogal co-author the Book of Wellness, a guide on basic hygiene, wound care and "herbs to avoid." Glade uses _mold earth_ to create a functional sewer and drainage system, which fixes the camp's overflowing latrines. Vogal invents a crude form of vasectomy by punching a guard.

The party hands the Book of Wellness to [[Sister Silas]] and [[Brother Ansel]] and establishes a rapport with the healers.

### The Aldani Shrine

Guided by Wulf Rygor, the party locates the abandoned Aldani Shrine. They find Lorsa Rygor and two others holding a barricade against the undead. The party engages the undead attackers and destroys them.

They trap a sentient undead minion. Instead of begging, the undead laughs and warns, _"The Hungerer comes..."_

## Aftermath

- The Book of Wellness, written by Taenae, Glade and Vogal, was left with Sister Silas.
- The rescue of Lorsa Rygor was in progress.
- Commander Breakbone was neutral. He was grateful for the sewer but demanding regarding Lorsa.
- Sister Silas was an ally, impressed by the party's medical knowledge and by Glade's civil engineering.
- Wulf Rygor was dependent on the party and desperate for them to save his wife.
