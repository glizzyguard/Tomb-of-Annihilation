# Session 21: Brothers, Bartering, and Betrayal

The party took [[Taenae]] back to [[The Dead Fish]] to sleep, and [[Inete]] joined them. At [[The Golden Serpent]] a performance by [[Vonara]] drew out the three older brothers of [[Taban Veilthorn|Taban]], and a formal fight between [[Vogal]] and [[Tharn Veilthorn]] was arranged at the [[Grand Colosseum]]. [[Glade]] found the sigil of the [[Zhentarim]] on the illusion rock and asked Inete about it, and she lied to him. The party spent the afternoon in the [[Grand Souk]] and returned to The Dead Fish for the night.

## Recap

### The changing of the guard

At midday on the twenty-second of Flamerule, 1492 DR, the party is outside the [[Temple of Savras]]. Taenae has hit rock bottom, and [[Zitembe]] has withdrawn his unconditional support.

Realizing that Taenae is in no state to adventure, the party escorts her back to The Dead Fish to sleep off her stupor.

Inete steps forward and joins the party. She cites terrifying visions of predators and of the jungle waking up. They are reminiscent of the dreams she had before meeting the party, but more urgent.

### The Golden Serpent

The party visits a high-class tavern in the [[00_Merchant_Ward|Merchant Ward]] called The Golden Serpent. Vonara dazzles the crowd with a performance and weaves a call for information on Taban into her act.

The performance draws the attention of three large men. The party finds out that they are Taban's older brothers: [[Korrak Veilthorn]], Tharn Veilthorn and [[Kaelar Veilthorn]].

The brothers claim that they do not know where Taban is. Vogal admits to sleeping with [[Kaveth]], the proprietor of The Dead Fish. This enrages Tharn, which implies a jealousy or a past relationship. Korrak de-escalates the immediate violence by arranging a formal fight between Vogal and Tharn at the Grand Colosseum. Kaelar spends the conversation openly flirting with Vonara.

### The secret of the stone

On the way to the Colosseum, Glade inspects the "illusion rock" found on the trash heap. He discovers a small sigil etched into it, a snake eating a coin. This is the symbol of the Zhentarim, the Black Network.

Glade asks Inete for guidance on the symbol. Despite knowing exactly what it is, Inete chooses to lie to him and claims ignorance.

### The Grand Souk

The party spends the afternoon perusing the stalls of the Grand Souk.

At [[Zarri's Blades and Bludgeons]], kept by [[Zarri]], a female Tabaxi, Vonara haggles for a new crossbow. By offering on-the-spot advertisement, a performance, she secures a bargain.

At [[The Gilded Scroll]], [[Eldrin Moonshadow]], a whimsical Elf, recognizes Inete as Zitembe's granddaughter. Upon learning of her path, he gifts her a scroll of _scorching ray_. Glade attempts to upgrade his Bloodwell Vial but is redirected to a specialist.

At [[Sorceries' Sundries]], [[Tyde Caller]], a female Halfling, quotes Glade 2,500 gold pieces to upgrade his current vial, which would take time, or 5,000 gold pieces for a brand new one. No purchase is made yet.

At [[The Jaunty Tune]], the proprietor is a genie who insists they are not a genie. Vogal orders a custom reptile skin hat of T-Rex leather. The price is a bet. If Vogal wins the duel, he pays 300 gold pieces and claims that the hat helped him win. If he loses the duel, he pays 450 gold pieces.

### The Dead Fish

The party returns to The Dead Fish to retire for the night. Taenae, who has recovered from her earlier stupor, Vogal and the proprietor Kaveth spend the night together. Aside from the tryst, the night passes peacefully for the rest of the group.

## Aftermath

- Inete joined the party.
- A formal fight between Vogal and Tharn at the Grand Colosseum was arranged and still to come.
- Taban was still missing. His brothers claimed not to know where he was.
- Taban's brothers were [[Korrak Veilthorn|Korrak the Unbroken]], [[Tharn Veilthorn|Tharn Bloodfist]], who was now Vogal's rival, and Kaelar Veilthorn.
- Eldrin Moonshadow, the scroll merchant, was an ally to Inete.
- Gained by Vonara: a light crossbow.
- Gained by Inete: a scroll of _scorching ray_.
- Gained by Glade: the Zhentarim stone.
- Vogal's hat was on order at The Jaunty Tune.
