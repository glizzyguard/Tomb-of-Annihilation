# Session 1: The Introduction

Five adventurers answered [[Syndra Silvane]]'s call to find the source of the [[The Death Curse|Death Curse]] and were sent by her magic to [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]]. There they took a room, gathered rumours, bought a dinosaur and a canoe, collected a gambling debt from the gladiator [[Taban Veilthorn]], who then joined them, and asked the [[Temple of Savras]] for guidance. [[Zitembe]], the head of the temple, began a long ritual on their behalf, and his granddaughter [[Inete]] paid to travel with them.

## Recap

### The commission

The party met Syndra Silvane and accepted her call to find the source of the Death Curse. Each of them had come to answer it alone, and they decided to face it together. Shortly afterwards Syndra teleported them to the [[00_Harbor_Ward|Harbor Ward]] of Port Nyanzaru. The city met them with brightly painted buildings and murals, the smell of spices and sea air, a musical, clicking language, and the noise of street performers and the working harbour.

### Kaya's House of Repose

On Syndra's recommendation the party took a shared room at [[Kaya's House of Repose]] for two gold pieces a night. Syndra went on to the villa of [[Wakanga O'tamu]], where she meant to stay for the rest of the adventure.

[[Vonara]] flirted with the bartender for information, and [[Vogal]], feeling a kinship with the half-orcs in the house, struck up a conversation with them. Between them they heard two rumours. The first was that five days by canoe up the [[Tiryki River]] a stone spire stood to the east, which the natives called [[Firefinger]]; "terror folk" nested there and would fight anyone they saw. The second was that the city of [[Mezro]] had not been destroyed in the Spellplague after all and that its ruins were an illusion. The teller had heard a pale-skinned man whisper this to a dinosaur man with a big sword, who smelled of honeysuckle.

### Shopping and a performance

[[Taenae]] bought a hadrosaurus from [[Ifan Talro'a]]. She had seen how the animal was treated and spent all of her gold on it, a hundred pieces. She named it [[Gooye]] and meant to take it into the jungle as a pack animal. The party also bought insect repellent, a strong-smelling salve of rendered dinosaur fat and menga leaves that kept off ordinary insects, two potions of healing, and a canoe, intending to go up either the Tiryki River or the [[Soshenstar River]].

Afterwards they explored more of the city and, inspired by the local tabaxi minstrels, stopped to put on a street performance together.

### Taban Veilthorn

[[K'lahu]], a Chultan thug and bookie at [[Executioner's Run]], hired the party to collect a gambling debt of five hundred gold from the gladiator Taban Veilthorn. They beat Taban into submission, and afterwards he joined them, meaning to restore his honour by helping to end the Death Curse. K'lahu paid them the agreed tenth, fifty gold, when they returned the full sum.

### The Temple of Savras

The party went to the Temple of Savras, god of wizards, divination, fate and truth, for guidance on the Death Curse. As they arrived they saw the head of the temple, Zitembe, turning away some red-robed figures and refusing them service. Asked about them, Zitembe said little. He offered instead to help find the source of the Death Curse, which would take a ritual sixteen hours long.

While they were there Zitembe's granddaughter, Inete, befriended the party. She offered a donation of a hundred gold to travel with them to the [[Aldani Basin]] to investigate the [[Red Wizards]] and the nightmare visions she had of a [[Snake Temple]].

The day ended on the thirtieth of Mirtul, 1492 DR. The party went back to Kaya's House to rest until the ritual was finished.

## Aftermath

- Taban Veilthorn joined the party.
- Inete joined the party.
- Zitembe's ritual was under way.
- Gained: Gooye the hadrosaurus, a canoe, insect repellent, two potions of healing, and fifty gold from K'lahu.
