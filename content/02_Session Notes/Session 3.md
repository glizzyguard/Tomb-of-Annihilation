# Session 3: The Serpent's Coil

The party infiltrated the [[Snake Temple]] in the [[Aldani Basin]], solved its tile puzzle and interrupted the ritual of the [[Red Wizards|Red Wizards of Thay]]. They fought the Avatar of the Serpent Death God and the wizards, who were slaughtered before they could fully become Yuan-ti. The party learned that the Death Curse was caused by a necrotic artifact called the [[Soulmonger]], took a hoard of coin, art objects and magic items, and camped for the night at the bottom of the temple.

## Recap

### The breach

The party had come to the Aldani Basin at [[Inete]]'s request to intercept a cell of the Red Wizards of Thay that was attempting to breach the Snake Temple. The party infiltrates the temple and solves the tile puzzle, which gives access to the inner sanctum.

### The ritual

The party interrupts the ritual. They fight the Avatar of the Serpent Death God and the Red Wizards, who are in the middle of their transformation. The Thayans are slaughtered, which prevents their full ascension to Yuan-ti, though the magic is volatile.

### The Soulmonger

From notes and journals that they find at the bottom of the shrine, the party learns that the cause of the Death Curse is a necrotic artifact called the Soulmonger.

### Taenae and Inete

In the heat of the moment [[Taenae]] tries to kiss Inete. The attempt fails, and awkwardness follows.

### Camp

Following their major combats, the party chooses to camp for the night at the bottom of the Snake Temple. The date is the fifth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.

## Aftermath

- The Red Wizards in the Snake Temple were killed and their ritual was interrupted.
- Inete was left awkward and confused. The failed romantic advance had complicated things between her and the party, though she remained grateful for the help against the Thayans.
- The Red Wizards of Thay held a vendetta. The party had now violently interfered with Thayan operations.
- Gained in coin: 80 gold, 1,100 silver and 2,200 copper pieces.
- Gained in art objects, worth about 175 gold in all: a pair of engraved bone dice, two small gold bracelets, two carved bone statuettes, a lute with a broken string and an obsidian knife with snake motifs.
- Gained in magic items: the [[Gem of Brightness]], called the "Sunlight Prism", the [[Staff of the Adder]] and the [[Cloak of Billowing]].
