# Session 2: Into the Green

[[Zitembe|Grandfather Zitembe]] finished his ritual and pointed the party toward the deep jungle. The party secured [[Azaka Stormfang]] as its guide, left [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]] and traveled for five days along the [[Soshenstar River]], where they discovered [[Camp Righteous]] and sighted the [[Heart of Ubtao]]. In the [[Aldani Basin]] they located the [[Snake Temple]] of [[Inete]]'s visions and prepared to infiltrate it.

## Recap

### The ritual

Grandfather Zitembe, who had begun a ritual at the [[Temple of Savras]], finishes his trance. He points the party toward the deep jungle and confirms the presence of a dark obelisk.

### Azaka Stormfang

The party secures the services of the guide Azaka Stormfang. She waives her fee in exchange for the party's promise to help her travel to [[Firefinger]] to retrieve a stolen item.

### The Soshenstar River

The party departs Port Nyanzaru and travels along the Soshenstar River. They discover Camp Righteous, which is abandoned and in a ruined state, and they sight the Heart of Ubtao floating in the distant sky. After five days of travel the date is the fifth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.

### The Aldani Basin

At Inete's behest the party arrives at the Aldani Basin. They locate the Snake Temple that Inete saw in her nightmare-visions and make preparations to infiltrate it and stop whatever dark ritual is being conducted.

## Aftermath

- Azaka Stormfang joined the party as its guide, a contracted ally. She expected the party to honor the Firefinger deal.
- Inete, an acolyte, was with the party as a companion.
- The [[Red Wizards]] were hostile. The party was actively interfering with their ritual.
- Gained: a hundred gold, a donation from Inete.
