# Session 8: The Pinnacle of Flame

[[Takhesis Calva]] and [[Vogal]] caught up with the party on [[Firefinger]], and the climb went on. In the third cave [[Fenro Silver]] burned the elder Pterafolk, and the party freed a bound Aarakocra named [[Nephyr]], who urged them to travel to [[Kir Sabal]]. At the top of the spire they defeated Nrak and his minions and rested, and they came away with coin, art objects and three magic items.

## Recap

### The reunion

Takhesis Calva and Vogal, who had lagged behind or scouted, catch up with the main group on the spire.

### The earth bridge

The party faces a massive gap in the path. [[Glade]] uses _mold earth_ to fashion a precarious bridge of rope and dirt. He almost falls to his death but is saved by the safety rope.

### The third cave

The party encounters the elder Pterafolk. Fenro Silver unleashes a spell resembling "napalm" and incinerates the elders. The sight of the burning corpses seems to give him grim inspiration for a painting. [[Vonara]] pockets a carved Human femur that is found in the nest.

### Nephyr

In the third cave the party finds a bound Aarakocra named Nephyr and rescues him. Nephyr urges the party to travel to Kir Sabal. He claims that its leader, [[Asharra]], can perform the Dance of the Seven Winds, which would grant the party magical flight.

### The pinnacle

The party reaches the top deck and engages Nrak and his minions. The victory is decisive. The Pterafolk are slaughtered. The party rests atop the spire, overlooking the jungle canopy.

These events fall on the twenty-seventh and twenty-eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.

## Aftermath

- Nephyr was an ally. He owed the party his life and was returning to Kir Sabal to spread word of their heroics.
- [[Azaka Stormfang]] was fulfilled. [[Azaka's Mask|Her mask]] was recovered and her quest was complete.
- Gained in coin: 50 platinum, 1,600 gold, 7,000 silver and 600 copper pieces.
- Gained in art objects, worth 1,000 gold in all: a large gold bracelet, a brass mug with jade inlay, a box of turquoise animal figurines and a carved ivory statuette.
- Gained in magic items: a [[Bag of Holding]], a [[Folding Boat]] and [[Quaal's Feather Token (Bird)]].
- Gained: five potions of healing.
