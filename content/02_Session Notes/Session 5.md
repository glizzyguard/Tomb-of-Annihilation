# Session 5: The Explosion & The Fumble

At the [[Temple of Savras]] the sick soldiers were stabilized and [[Zitembe|Grandfather Zitembe]] confirmed that the [[Soulmonger]] was the artifact causing the Death Curse. [[Taenae]] offended [[Inete]] with the gift of a holy symbol. The party then traveled toward [[Firefinger]], lost its canoe in rapids on the [[Tiryki River]], was ambushed by su-monsters and discovered a dead beholder.

## Recap

### The Temple of Savras

The sick soldiers, whom the party had escorted from [[Camp Vengeance]] to [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]], are stabilized at the Temple of Savras. Inete warmly welcomes the party back and even hugs Taenae. Grandfather Zitembe confirms that the Soulmonger, a crafted necrotic artifact, is the specific artifact causing the curse.

### Shopping

[[Vogal]] demonstrates his jump height; whether for intimidation or for flair is not recorded. [[Vonara]] secures rations and holy items. Taenae returns to give Inete a holy symbol but has forgotten Inete's deity. Inete snatches the amulet, offended, and tells them to leave.

### The Tiryki River

The party travels toward Firefinger. While they navigate the tumultuous rapids on the Tiryki River, [[Glade]] casts _earth tremor_ in an attempt to stabilize the boat and solve a navigation problem. The canoe is destroyed. Glade and Vonara fall in, and Taenae, in the form of a giant octopus, saves them. [[Takhesis Calva]] flies to safety carrying Vogal.

### The su-monsters

Su-monsters, psychic monkeys, ambush the party. Vonara and Glade nearly die. They are saved by Vogal and Takhesis Calva, and by Taenae, who takes the form of a brown bear.

### The dead beholder

The party discovers a dead beholder being eaten by vultures. Vonara scares the vultures off. Vogal spots a trail but decides not to follow it. The date is the seventh of Kythorn, 1492 DR.

## Aftermath

- Inete was offended. Taenae's forgetfulness about her faith was a major faux pas. Inete returned to her place in the Temple of Savras.
- [[Azaka Stormfang]] was impatient. Watching the party blow up their own boat had raised questions about their competence.
- Lost: the canoe.
