# Session 20: The Morning After

[[Vonara]], [[Glade]] and [[Takhesis Calva|Takhesis]] woke at [[The Dead Fish]] and, on the way to the [[Temple of Savras]], found [[Taenae]] passed out on a trash heap, robbed. Glade found a magical rock there that projected the illusion of a tavern. At the temple [[Zitembe]] received the party coldly. Word of [[Inete]]'s humiliation had reached him, and he would finish the tasks [[Vogal]] had paid for and offer nothing more.

## Recap

### Rock bottom

It is the morning of the twenty-second of Flamerule, 1492 DR, at The Dead Fish, a tavern. The party is waking after a night of revelry and spiraling. Taenae is missing. Vogal spent the night pursuing [[Kaveth]].

Vonara, Glade and Takhesis wake up at The Dead Fish and immediately set out to regroup at the Temple of Savras.

On the way, they discover Taenae passed out on a trash heap, completely destitute. She was robbed of her coin and dignity during the night.

Glade investigates the scene. Taenae believes she was drinking at a specific establishment. Glade finds a magical rock embedded in the ground that projects an illusion of a tavern. Taenae was lured into a trap, or a cruel magical prank, and left to rot. She had not been partying.

### The cold shoulder

The group, dragging a recovering Taenae, arrives at the Temple of Savras.

Zitembe greets them, but the warmth is gone. His demeanor is cold and strictly business-like. Word of Inete's humiliation, from Taenae's proposition, has reached him. He makes it clear that he will complete the tasks Vogal has already paid for, finding [[Taban Veilthorn|Taban]], but that he will offer no further personal aid, sanctuary or friendship.

## Aftermath

- Zitembe was cold and transactional. The alliance was fractured because of Taenae's actions.
- Inete stayed away, likely avoiding the party.
- Taenae was suffering from the aftermath of her spiral.
