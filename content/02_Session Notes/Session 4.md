# Session 4: The River Run

The party traveled back up the [[Aldani Basin]], found four teleportation circles they could not yet use, and was ambushed by a giant crocodile. At [[Camp Vengeance]] they agreed with [[Niles Breakbone|Commander Breakbone]] to carry seven sick soldiers and a guard named [[Adam]] to [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]]. On the river they escaped a tyrannosaurus rex, and they delivered the soldiers to the [[Temple of Savras]].

## Recap

### The Aldani Basin

During the travel back up the Aldani Basin, [[Glade]] puts on a show that references _Dragons and Houses_.

The party finds four teleportation circles scattered on their map or route. Lacking the arcane knowledge, they cannot yet decipher the sigil sequences needed to use them.

### The giant crocodile

A giant crocodile ambushes the party. [[Taban Veilthorn|Taban]] is nearly killed. [[Taenae]] takes the form of a giant octopus. [[Vogal]] finds that crocodiles do not have kneecaps, much to his frustration. The party eventually dispatches the crocodile following a combined effort.

### Camp Vengeance

The party arrives at Camp Vengeance and encounters Commander Breakbone. Instead of fighting or politicking, they agree to transport seven sick soldiers of the Order of the Gauntlet, who are suffering from throat leeches, and a guard named [[Adam]] back to Port Nyanzaru.

### The tyrannosaurus

On the river return a rampaging tyrannosaurus rex pursues the party. Vogal scouts the threat, and Glade and Vogal use their combined immense strength to paddle the canoe at breakneck speed. A wild brontosaurus is left behind to satisfy the tyrannosaurus's hunger, and the party escapes unharmed.

### Port Nyanzaru

The party delivers the sick soldiers to the Temple of Savras, where [[Zitembe|Grandfather Zitembe]] and [[Inete]] work to cure them. The date is the sixth of Kythorn, 1492 DR.

## Aftermath

- The seven sick soldiers were delivered to the Temple of Savras.
- [[Niles Breakbone]] was grudgingly grateful. The party had solved a logistical nightmare for him by removing the sick troops.
- [[Adam]], the guard, was an ally. He owed the party his life and health.
- The four teleportation circles were left undeciphered.
