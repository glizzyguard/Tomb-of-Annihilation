# Session 19: Smoke, Skoon, and The Dead Fish

In [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]] the party regrouped at the [[Temple of Savras]] and told [[Zitembe|Grandfather Zitembe]] of [[The Hungerer|the Hungerer]]'s final words, and Zitembe promised to research the lost city of Mezro. In the [[Grand Souk]] they bought tobacco from a salesman named [[Begär]]. They spent the night at a tavern called [[The Dead Fish]], where [[Vogal]] beat the Half-Orc [[Kragga]] at arm wrestling and failed her challenge to drink three cups of Skoon, and [[Vonara]] pressed the pickpocket [[Pip Proudfoot]] into service to find word of [[Taban Veilthorn|Taban]].

## Recap

### The temple

The party is in Port Nyanzaru. They have returned from the jungle to regroup, to seek information on Taban and to identify the next step in the investigation of the Death Curse. It is the twenty-first of Flamerule, 1492 DR, in the evening.

[[Taenae]] has gone off alone to drink. The rest of the party regroups at the Temple of Savras.

They debrief with Grandfather Zitembe and relay the Hungerer's final words: _"Mezro shall keep me safe."_ Zitembe promises to research the lost city and its connection to the entity, and to reach out to contacts regarding Soul Magic.

[[Inete]] keeps her distance after Taenae's failed proposition.

### The Grand Souk

The party visits the Grand Souk, the city's grandest bazaar. There they meet Begär, a charismatic salesman of fine tobacco and smoking accessories. He is the Beggar King in disguise. Vogal buys a lighter and 20 rations of hookah. Vonara buys everyday cigarettes and a set of "very fine" cigarettes in a fancy container that doubles as a lighter.

### The Dead Fish

The party retires to a tavern in the Merchant Ward called The Dead Fish. The group theorizes about Taban's location and motives.

Vogal challenges Kragga, a massive female Half-Orc, to an arm wrestling match and wins the contest. Kragga promises to teach him "how to cleave" enemies.

Kragga challenges Vogal to drink Skoon, a volatile Chultan spirit. The test is to drink three cups. Vogal gets the first two down but fails on the third, sputtering flames and burning himself in front of the tavern.

On returning to his table, Vogal notices a thief working the room. Vonara confronts the pickpocket, a Halfling by the name of Pip Proudfoot. Instead of turning him in, she intimidates him into service and tasks him with finding information on Taban in the city's underbelly.

### Night calls

The party rents rooms for the night. Vogal flirts with [[Kaveth]], the tall, thin Half-Orc proprietor of the tavern, and the flirting is well received.

Vonara goes swiftly to sleep. [[Takhesis Calva|Takhesis]] goes for a solitary nighttime stroll to clear his head. Vogal remains at the bar, waiting for Kaveth to finish her shift.

## Aftermath

- Zitembe was researching Mezro.
- Taenae and Inete remained distant, and Taenae had not come back by the time the party slept.
- Pip Proudfoot was in Vonara's service as an informant, looking for word of Taban.
- Kragga, who respected strength, had promised to teach Vogal how to cleave enemies.
- Kaveth was Vogal's romantic interest.
- Gained by Vogal: a lighter and 20 rations of hookah.
- Gained by Vonara: everyday cigarettes, and a set of very fine cigarettes in a container that doubled as a lighter.
