# Glade

**Glade**, called Glade of the Forest and afterwards Glade of the Jungle, was a wood elf sorcerer of draconic blood from [[The Blackthorn Forest]] and one of the five adventurers who answered [[Syndra Silvane]]'s call to [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]].[^bs][^s1][^s51] He served the expedition as its shaper of earth and bodies and its keeper of teleportation circles, and he carried the [[Heartwell Vial]] into the [[Tomb of the Nine Gods]].[^s12][^s28][^s83] He survived the expedition and remained in Chult afterwards.[^s91]

> [!quote] Glade, over the body of the Boy
> _"Sometimes you leave friends in the forest, and sometimes you step around them to find new paths."_[^s88]

## Description

Glade was a wood elf who stood five feet nine inches, slender, with tanned skin, long ears, forest-green eyes and wispy, curly dark brown hair. He wore traveler's robes in the colors of local plants, hung with twigs and leaves.

From the fifteenth of Eleasis his right hand was grave-cold to the wrist, and plants leaned away from it.[^s83][^s91]

## Personality

Glade habitually spoke in metaphors drawn from the forest, which his companions did not always understand.[^s86][^s87] On being possessed by [[Kubazan]] he declared that he felt like the strongest tree in the forest, as the forest is fed by the water.[^s74] [[The Boy]] told him, _"You're even more weird than I thought, Glade."_[^s87]

He was attentive to companions in difficulty. He remained at the Boy's cage while the others searched the room,[^s87] and he reassured [[Inete]] when she was afraid to go further into the Tomb.[^s65]

He enjoyed performing and entertained the party on the road with a show about dragons and houses.[^s4] He smoked a twenty-year-aged leaf he called Heirwon, and spent his nights in contemplation of soul magic.

## Abilities

Glade was a sorcerer whose magic came from draconic ancestry.[^bs][^s69] The ancestry gave him a knowledge of dragons and allowed him to parley with one.[^s51][^s85]

His magic first showed itself in the shaping of earth,[^bs] and he used _mold earth_ for engineering throughout the expedition. Examples include a bridge of rope and dirt on [[Firefinger]] and a working sewer for [[Camp Vengeance]].[^s8][^s13] He was later able to cast _move earth_.[^s63]

He frequently cast _polymorph_ on himself and his companions. The forms he used included giant apes, tyrannosaurs and baboons.[^s28][^s45][^s57]

He could operate teleportation circles, and used those at [[Kir Sabal]] and [[The Bastion]] to move the party across Chult.[^s12][^s50][^s91]

Within the Tomb he made repeated use of _telekinesis_, with which he halted machinery, retrieved objects from trapped rooms and stopped a stone juggernaut.[^s66][^s72][^s77][^s84] Other spells he was recorded casting include _haste_, _dispel magic_, _sunbeam_, _blink_ and _disintegrate_.[^s54][^s64][^s78][^s87][^s90]

While possessed by Kubazan he had strength far beyond his own.[^s74]

## Possessions

- [[Ring of Spell Turning]] and [[Wand of the Blackthorn Forest]], taken from a statue made in his likeness beneath the Hungerer's throne.[^s17]
- The [[Bloodwell Vial]], which became the [[Heartwell Vial]]. See History.
- A fist-sized geode with a core of trapped starlight and volcanic veins, which he woke holding after a night of visions in the Bastion.[^s35]
- Two stone golem cores, one from the yuan-ti warehouse in the Harbor Ward and one taken on the way out of the [[Maw Below]],[^s27][^s28] and a manual of stone golems taken from [[Withers]]' office.[^s68]
- A magical shield recovered from the undead tyrannosaur in the [[Refuse Pit]].[^s45] His shield was the [[Scale of the Sun-Weaver]], a slab of petrified scale from his ancestor.
- The Bracers of Archery, the vessel of Kubazan.[^s74]
- The [[Eye of Zaltec]], a flawless fist-sized ruby.[^s84]
- A pot of soil, able to grow all, the Boy's gift to him.[^s88]
- The [[Living Orchid Necklace]], which opened every petal when the Tomb fell.[^s90] It was a Blackthorn orchid on a vine whose petals wilted when he was wounded.

## Relationships

[[Aurellion the Sun-Weaver]], a gold dragon, was his ancestor.

[[Savriel Tirinath]] confided in Glade that she did not expect to leave the Tomb alive, and that she was already struggling to keep up.[^s86] She had witnessed his working at the river of souls and confirmed his reading of its magic.[^s83]

[[The Boy]] gave Glade the instructions for the green gate and recited to him a saying of Glade's own: _"Sometimes the most beautiful scenery on the forest path is the one who walks by your side."_[^s87]

[[Inete]] lied to Glade about a [[Zhentarim]] sigil he asked her to identify,[^s21] and a hag impersonating her cost him a thousand gold pieces.[^s42][^s43] He carried her body out of the Cradle and to the camp where she was raised.[^s90][^s91]

[[Takhesis Calva]] fought beside Glade as a second giant ape in the Maw Below.[^s28] During the party's first rest in the Tomb, Glade tried to speak with him and was answered, _"The dungeon is perfect in its design."_ Takhesis was taken from the party that morning.[^s70]

[[Tyde Caller]] of [[Sorceries' Sundries]] advised Glade on golem cores and took the commission to remake his vial. The two shared a reverence for nature, his for the forest and hers for the sea.[^s21][^s22][^s29][^s45]

[[Vogal]] received Glade's magical help in his duel with [[Tharn Veilthorn]] and bought him a gift in thanks.[^s27][^s30]

[[Taenae]] named Glade as her favorite when the voice in the lake asked.[^s85]

[[Kubazan]] possessed Glade from the fourteenth of Eleasis until the Tomb fell.[^s74][^s90]

[[Drakar]] and [[Urzok]], gladiators of the [[Grand Colosseum]], were hired by Glade to defend the Bastion and to teach him the shield.[^s48]

## History

### Before the expedition

Glade was born into [[The Collective]] of the Blackthorn Forest, in the clearing after which he was named, and was raised by its guardians. His magic appeared in adolescence, and as a young adult he used it to defend the Collective and tend its trees. He left after a few decades, when he found a tree of his childhood blighted and pulsing with necrotic energy, and he regarded the journey as a way of protecting his home. He took the name "of the Forest" on the dock as he departed.[^bs] His own account is given in full under Backstory.

### Chult and Valachan

On the thirtieth of Mirtul, 1492 DR, Glade and four others accepted Syndra Silvane's commission to find the source of the Death Curse and were sent by her magic to Port Nyanzaru.[^s1] On the seventh of Kythorn he destroyed the party's canoe in rapids on the [[Tiryki River]] with _earth tremor_.[^s5][^s80]

At [[Ataaz Muhahah]] the party was taken into the mists of [[Valachan]], where each woke in a stranger's body.[^s9] Glade's was Atzi, a squire of Lathander, who was burned to death by a mage on the second day. Glade woke in Chult with the others.[^s10][^s11][^s12]

At Kir Sabal he woke the monastery's dormant teleportation circle and sent the party to the [[Aldani Basin]].[^s12] At Camp Vengeance he wrote the [[The Book of Wellness|Book of Wellness]] with Taenae and Vogal and dug the camp's sewer, which had visibly reduced its sickness by the time the party returned from the [[Aldani Shrine]].[^s13][^s18]

### Port Nyanzaru and the Bastion

In the city Glade found the magic stone that had projected a false tavern around Taenae, and found on it a sigil of a snake eating a coin, the mark of the Zhentarim.[^s20][^s21] At the gala of the twenty-third of Flamerule he and [[Vonara]] accused [[Jessamine]] of a poisoning. She fled, and the party later learned that the poisoner had been an agent of [[Ras Nsi]] and that her escape had delivered her to the yuan-ti.[^s23][^s29]

On the twenty-fifth, after Takhesis and Taenae were killed in the Maw Below and returned by their patrons, Glade turned himself and Takhesis into giant apes and broke a way out.[^s28] On the twenty-seventh, at the [[The Bastion|Sunken Temple]], he found the [[Eye of Ubtao]] in the mechanism of the scales, which gave the party control of the temple.[^s33] That night he dreamed of a shipwreck cove and woke holding the geode.[^s35]

On the thirty-first a night hag impersonating Inete tried to steal from [[Green Things, Potions & More]] in his company, and Glade paid a thousand gold pieces in damages.[^s42][^s43][^s44] On the second of Eleasis he and Taenae cleared the Refuse Pit of the [[Temple of Euphoria]], where he cut the heart from an undead tyrannosaur. He gave the heart and eight thousand gold to Tyde Caller to remake the Bloodwell Vial.[^s45]

The settlement at the Bastion was founded as [[Tawb]] on the fourth of Eleasis.[^s49] He sat on its ruling council and kept the Bastion's teleportation circle, archive and meditation chamber.

### Omu

Glade keyed the Bastion's circle to Kir Sabal for the journey south.[^s50] In the [[Valley of Embers]] he obtained directions to [[Omu]] from the dragon [[Tamborus]].[^s51] In Omu he took part in recovering the puzzle cubes of I'jin and Wongo, entering [[Wongo's Shrine]] as a baboon with Inete.[^s56][^s57]

In the [[Fane of the Night Serpent]] he posed as a convert and helped arm the slave revolt.[^s59][^s60] Afterwards he sealed the breach made by the King of Feathers, trapping the beast inside.[^s63]

### The Tomb of the Nine Gods

The party entered the Tomb on the thirteenth of Eleasis.[^s64] On the first level the mummy of Wongo attacked Glade and tried to force its mace on him, which he resisted.[^s66] On the third level he took up the Bracers of Archery and was possessed by Kubazan.[^s74] In the maze of [[Unkh]] on the fourth level he inserted the green key while the sarcophagus glowed purple, losing the key, and in the fight that followed he and Vonara twice revived [[Ersklan Ghostfang]] before he was killed.[^s75]

On the fifteenth he signed [[Withers]]' contract with the rest of the party and fought the beholder [[Belchorzh]].[^s77][^s78] He then freed Savriel Tirinath by shattering the [[Mirror of Life Trapping]].[^s79]

On the fifth level a skeleton gardener gave him the last of the skeleton keys, its own skull. The Bloodwell Vial was by then in his pack with a note from Tyde Caller: _"It needs to drink from death that flows... You'll know the place when the heart knocks."_ How the vial was returned to him is not recorded. He held it for a minute in the river of souls that ran down through the Tomb. The vial became the Heartwell, which beat faster the nearer it came to the Soulmonger, and his right hand was left permanently cold.[^s83]

On the sixteenth he stopped the juggernaut with _telekinesis_ and received the Eye of Zaltec.[^s84] On the sixth level he completed two of the five trials of the green gate.[^s87] During the rest that the Boy gave the party, the sixth circle of his magic returned to him; when he had lost it is not recorded.[^s88]

### The Cradle

In the Cradle the Heartwell Vial pointed at the Soulmonger, and Glade's explanation led Taenae to identify it as the source of the Death Curse.[^s89] He and Taenae together broke one of the three struts that held it.[^s89]

When Takhesis rose on wings of ice, Glade was the only one of the party frightened. He and Inete each cast _disintegrate_ at Takhesis without effect.[^s90] After Takhesis beheaded Inete, Glade lifted her body with _telekinesis_ and carried it through the arch as the Cradle was destroyed.[^s90] As the party left the Tomb the Heartwell Vial beat once and stopped, and a golden shape looked back at him.[^s90]

### Epilogue

The party woke in the jungle before dawn on the seventeenth of Eleasis. Glade carried Inete's body to the camp, where [[Sister Silas]] raised her.[^s91] The vial no longer beat.[^s91]

Glade did not return to the Blackthorn Forest. He set off in search of the next quest, no longer Glade of the Forest but Glade of the Jungle, a protector of Chult as a whole, perhaps having felt his ancestral spirit drawn there.[^s91]

### Backstory

_The following is Glade's backstory as written by Adam, his player, in full._

> The forest whispers, and a child is born.
>
> The Collective of the Blackthorn Forest welcomed Glade into the world when the weather was mild, the small clearing after which he was named was already lush with growth and life. It was in this peaceful and protected meadow that Glade grew up, raised to appreciate and honor the life sprouting all around him by the rotating group of guardians that helped raise the children given to the Collective.
>
> As Glade’s ears began to point with adolescence, something odd began to occur. As the young wood elf worked diligently in the garden, his guardian noticed that the earth beneath his fingertips would seem to move mere moments before his hands would touch the ground. These were the first signs of what would become young Glade’s signature spell, though the magic that flowed through his veins held power far beyond the simple moulding of earth.
>
> Magic users in the Collective were few and far between, but the dwellers of the forest had old stories of an ancient guardian, a towering bronze dragon as wizened as the tallest trees, and how the generations of the forest were speckled with its descendants.
>
> Glade spent his young adulthood building his innate magical skills and using them to aid the Collective. More than one band of would be robbers found themselves running away from pine-nettle shaped magic missiles; and the great Blackthorn trees seemed to grow taller and stronger every season after Glade massaged their roots with giant tremors in the earth.
>
> One night, after Glade had spent the twilight hours trying to weave his magic into a spindly hand of branches that could pick fruit off the highest trees, a rustle outside caught his attention. As he watched leaves blow past his window, Glade could swear he saw a flash of gold veins shine quickly in the moonlight. His interest peaked, Glade rushed out into the night, following where the wind would take him. The gold veined leaf would flit in and out of view, always at the edge of his vision, leading him deeper and deeper into the forest. When the moon had just reached its highest point Glade suddenly stopped cold, all thoughts of the gilded leaf gone, for he had just laid eyes on something he had never seen in the forest before: death.
>
> Things died in the forest all the time; such was the way of life. But what Glade saw before him was something far other than the decaying remains of a woodland creature, or charcoaled splinters of once beautiful greenery taken by forest fire. Glade sank to his knees as he looked upon what used to be one of his favorite trees to climb as a child, now shrunken and mangled with blight. And Glade’s natural connection to the arcane seemed more a curse to him in this moment, for he felt this once great pillar of his upbringing, a symbol of his forest, pulsing with a dark, necrotic energy.
>
> • • •
>
> Glade stood in front of the gathered Collective in the early dawn’s light; faces that helped raise him, faces he had protected for years, looked back at him with mixtures of pride and sadness. They had worked tirelessly to help gather the supplies now strapped to his back, to weave the travel cloak that adorned his shoulders when he told them his plans to leave. And though he had attended many parting rituals during his few decades in the forest, he never thought he would feel called to leave himself.
>
> Glade reminded himself that he wasn’t abandoning his home as the small port town at the edge of the Blackthorn Forest came into view through the trees. By embarking on his journey, Glade was protecting his home. And he could feel great magic and adventure that awaited him at the end of this mystery.
>
> “Wot’s the name fo’ the ticket?” Glade was torn from his thoughts as he handed a copper coin to the boatswain. Waves lapped against the dock, and the wood elf felt out of place without trees towering above him.
>
> “Glade…” The answer came instinctively, the only way he had ever been addressed by others in the Collective, the single syllable that named the small clearing where he was born. That thought made him look over his shoulder one last time, where he could still see a dark green canopy now almost distant past the town and the shore.
>
> “Of the Forest.” He added as he turned back, deciding to carry his home with him, if only in one small way.
