# The Party

The player characters of the expedition to Chult, and the companions who traveled with them. Each entry names the character's player.

## The five who answered Syndra Silvane

- [[Vogal]], Orc barbarian and Champion of Nyanzaru, played by Cam. With the party from Session 1 to Session 91.[^s1][^s91]
- [[Vonara]], Half-Elf bard, played by Von. With the party from Session 1 to Session 91.[^s1][^s91]
- [[Glade]], Wood Elf sorcerer, played by Adam. With the party from Session 1 to Session 91.[^s1][^s91]
- [[Taenae]], Tabaxi druid and priestess of Chakuna, played by Danielle. With the party from Session 1 to Session 91.[^s1][^s91]
- [[Takhesis Calva]], Fallen Aasimar fighter, played by Coop. With the party from Session 1 until he was taken in Session 70, and against it in Sessions 89 and 90, where he was killed.[^s1][^s70][^s89][^s90]

## Those who joined later

- [[Inete]], Eladrin wizard of the Temple of Savras, played by Nibbles. She traveled with the party as a companion in Sessions 3 and 4 and joined as a player character in Session 18. She was killed in Session 90 and raised in Session 91.[^s18][^s90][^s91]
- [[Ersklan Ghostfang]], called Fang, Human rogue, played by Coop. He joined in Session 70 and was killed in Session 75.[^s70][^s75]
- [[Raiketsu]], Goliath monk, played by Coop. He joined in Session 76 and was killed in Session 89.[^s76][^s89]

## Companions

- [[Taban Veilthorn]], a gladiator who joined on the expedition's first day and left it after Valachan, run by Patrick, the Dungeon Master.[^s1][^s12]
- [[Azaka Stormfang]], who traveled with the party from Session 2 until her mask was recovered at Firefinger, run by Patrick.[^s2][^s8]
- [[Fenro Silver]], a sorcerer who joined Vogal and Vonara in the jungle and went with the party to Firefinger, played by DJ as a guest.[^s6][^s8]
- [[Alice]], who traveled with the party from Session 50 to Session 55.[^s50][^s55]
- [[Withers]], the overseer of the Tomb, who made a contract with the party in Session 77 and helped it afterward. A very temporary companion.[^s77]
- [[Savriel Tirinath]], a Watcher freed from the Mirror of Life Trapping, run by Patrick. She traveled with the party from Session 80 and stayed behind in the Cradle.[^s80][^s90]
- [[Snib]], an underclerk of the Tomb, played by Alfie as a guest in Session 81 and killed by Vogal in Session 82.[^s81][^s82]
