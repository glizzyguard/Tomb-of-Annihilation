# Master Noke

**Master Noke** was a wizard of [[Noke's Keep]] who had transformed the wizard [[Finethir Shinebright]] into a dinosaur, sent his enforcers to recover him, and died when his own wand backfired.[^s6]

## Possessions

- The Wand of True Polymorph, which was destroyed in restoring Finethir Shinebright to his true form.[^s6]

## Relationships

Finethir Shinebright was a wizard whom Noke had transformed into a dinosaur and kept in that form. Noke's people called him the "sheep."[^s6]

Guz, a Half-Orc, was his enforcer and came for Finethir with wolves and a golem.[^s6]

## History

On the eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, Guz arrived at Noke's Keep with wolves and a golem and demanded of [[Vogal]], [[Vonara]] and the sorcerer [[Fenro Silver]] that Finethir be returned to Master Noke. They refused, and Guz and his wolves were cut down. What became of the golem is not recorded.[^s6]

Noke then appeared himself, wielding the Wand of True Polymorph. Desperate to crush the three, he tried to use the wand to turn himself into a wyvern. The wand backfired, and his body imploded under the strain.[^s6]
