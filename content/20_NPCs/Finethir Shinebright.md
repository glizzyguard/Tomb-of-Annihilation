# Finethir Shinebright

**Finethir Shinebright** was a wizard whom [[Master Noke]] had trapped in the form of a dinosaur, and whom [[Vogal]], [[Vonara]] and the sorcerer [[Fenro Silver]] defended from Noke's enforcers at [[Noke's Keep]].[^s6]

## Description

In his trapped form Finethir was a Ti-Khana deinonychus, a magical mutation of a deinonychus, a carnivorous species of dinosaur. Vonara recognized the creature's Yuan-ti origins.[^s6] His true form is not recorded.

## History

Master Noke transformed Finethir into a dinosaur and kept him in that form.

On the eighth of Kythorn, 1492 DR, Finethir burst in on Vogal, Vonara and Fenro Silver at Noke's Keep, chased by wolves. By means of a scroll he introduced himself as a wizard trapped in the form of a beast. Guz, a Half-Orc enforcer, then arrived with wolves and a golem and demanded that the "sheep" be returned to Master Noke. The three refused and fought, and Guz and his wolves were cut down.[^s6]

Master Noke appeared with the Wand of True Polymorph and died when it backfired on him. The wand was destroyed in restoring Finethir to his true form. Once he was himself again, Finethir told Vonara and the others that Noke had been the one who transformed him. He owed the three a life debt. Nothing further is recorded of him.[^s6]
