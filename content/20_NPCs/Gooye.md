# Gooye

**Gooye** was a hadrosaurus that [[Taenae]] bought in [[00_Port Nyaznaru_Map|Port Nyanzaru]] as a pack animal and later left in the care of the Aarakocra of [[Kir Sabal]].[^s1][^s12]

## History

On the thirtieth of Mirtul, 1492 DR, Taenae bought the hadrosaurus from [[Ifan Talro'a]] in Port Nyanzaru. She had seen how the animal was treated and spent all of her gold on it, a hundred pieces. She named it Gooye and meant to take it into the jungle as a pack animal.[^s1]

On the road to [[Firefinger]] late in Kythorn the party found two triceratops stuck in a tar pit. [[Vogal]] tied ropes to Gooye, and Gooye pulled them free. When the party came to a massive gorge it took the long way around to save him.[^s7]

On the thirtieth of Kythorn Taenae left Gooye with the Aarakocra of Kir Sabal, who were to watch over him, because she was worried about taking him into danger.[^s12] She had a joyful reunion with him when the party came back to Kir Sabal early in Eleasis.[^s50]
